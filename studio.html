01 · The writing

The Deliberate

On the craft of operating well inside complex systems.

Most writing about work hands you frameworks to apply and tactics to copy. The Deliberate is something different: a newsletter for people trying to think carefully about how they work—and why it matters.

Each issue explores the intersection of good business and good life: how work design shapes the texture of daily experience, how individuals build the capacity to act with agency inside complex systems, and what it looks like to treat leadership as a craft worth developing deliberately.

It’s for change agents and organizational practitioners, for leaders who read more fiction than business books, for people suspicious of business-as-usual advice who still believe organizations can be genuinely better.

Much of what eventually becomes an essay on this site—and a fair amount that never will—appears there first. If you want a feel for the writing before subscribing, the archive is the best sampler.
